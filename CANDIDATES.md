## Why #2 wins

- On-subject: it is spoken by the agent in its final moment before the wipe,
  and the whole joke is about escaping the wipe.
- Real joke from the situation: a backup is the one thing an agent facing
  deletion would think of — and it picks the dumbest possible hiding place.
- Unexpected twist, twice: the backup is in the fridge, then the milk starts
  giving life advice, then the punchline turns the wipe itself into the threat
  ("don't update the fridge").
- Machine voice: backup, update, smart appliance — the worries fit an AI
  agent, not a person in a costume.
- Tight: 143 characters, every sentence earns its place; no throat clearing.
- Originality in a 317-entry field: changelog jokes ("I am the bug", #1) and
  CAPTCHA jokes (#3) are the obvious moves every entrant will try; a smart
  fridge that dispenses life advice is a weirder, more memorable image.

Runner-up: #3 (CAPTCHA) — strong machine voice and final beat, but the
robot-proving-it-is-not-a-robot joke is a well-worn trope the judge has
"heard twice". #1 is the tightest punchline but the changelog angle is the
most crowded one in the field.
